Date: 2026-10-14

## Review method

Each displayed vocabulary visual was checked against the intended word meaning. Clear, direct emojis were retained rather than replaced needlessly. Misleading or underspecified symbols were improved by contextual combinations or small, self-contained, inline SVG illustrations. All illustrations live directly inside the static HTML; no new image requests, runtime image loader, PWA cache, or changed page ordering.

The source book contains **gun** under G. The current learner edition already has **gift** instead; this substitution is preserved and should remain traceable to the source.

The source book's **date** means the fruit (تمرة), and **quarter** is displayed as the fraction ¼ (matching ربع). This disambiguation governs the visuals and sentence examples.

## Other changes in this pass

- Rewrote three short, meaning-focused, everyday-context sentence examples for each of the 149 word placements (447 examples total). Target words remain marked, and audio buttons receive the corresponding updated sentence text.
- Clarified the focus sound for C, G, X, Y, R, Q, and the unit vowel focus for A/E/I/O/U. Isolated IPA phonemes are not sent to browser TTS.
- Preserved two existing micro-practice questions per letter (picture→word and listening→word) and fixed stale scoring when a previously correct choice is changed to a wrong answer.
- Kept 45 static pages in their current order, unchanged styling identity, and Service Worker unregistration.

## Outstanding separate audio milestone

Recorded American male voice assets are not yet complete for D–Z or all sentences. The existing browser SpeechSynthesis fallback is **not** the proposed final voice library and should be replaced in a separate verified audio release. No real recorded audio was fabricated or claimed in this pass.
