## Editorial standards
- `who` as an object is also widely accepted in informal register; `whom` is not the only grammatical object pronoun in all varieties.
- `that` can be used for people as well as things in defining relative clauses; not usually in nondefining clauses after a comma.
- `whose` signals possession and precedes the possessed noun.
- The book's mnemonic patterns are heuristics, not exhaustive grammatical rules.
