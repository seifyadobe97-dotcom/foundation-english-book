## Editorial principles
1. Keep category names and original source IDs intact in the private classification table. Leave `mapping_status='needs_review'` until a real teaching home is implemented and reviewed; never silently remap a topic to an unrelated lesson.
2. Add instructor-created examples for demonstration where the approved source is insufficient; **do not** pass them as source-original scored assessment.
3. Before grading Sentence Insertion, Sentence Combining, Logical Completion or Correct Sentence, audit sentence-level grammar, coreference, chronological coherence, option uniqueness and textual errors — do not rely on printed highlighting as a substitute for validation.
4. Separate communicative grammar from discourse/paragraph-coherence and from spelling. Those topics may warrant a distinct Writing/Language skill group when the course roadmap expands, not indiscriminate inclusion in the Grammar sequence.
5. Preserve the current protected pilot responses, historical attempts and completed lesson publications. The foundation module at position 999 remains closed.
