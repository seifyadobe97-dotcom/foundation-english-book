## Editorial distinctions
- `because + clause` vs `owing to + noun phrase`.
- `so` for result vs `so that` for purpose.
- `but` vs `although`, and `in spite of + noun/-ing`.
- `while` for simultaneous events; future reference in time clauses usually uses present simple.
- `First`, `In addition`, `For example` have distinct logical functions.
