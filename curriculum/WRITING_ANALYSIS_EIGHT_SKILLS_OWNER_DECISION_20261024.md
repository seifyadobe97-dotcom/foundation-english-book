## Release and safety controls

- The eight indicators are currently **drafts**. This decision does not publish any Writing Analysis lesson or question to students. Publication requires source audit, complete explanation stages, approved items, and a successful course release gate.
- Original bank questions and answer keys remain in private Supabase storage. The public repo stores taxonomy metadata only, never the protected question text.
- Do not change existing course codes, question IDs, simulation `origin`, students' scores or attempts. Any future reassignment must use the reasoned owner-only audited workflow.
- The Admin curriculum page labels the five book chapters versus three additional bank skills. Student cards only describe published-ready lessons.
- Grammar lesson count, its established order, and the paused foundation remain unchanged.
