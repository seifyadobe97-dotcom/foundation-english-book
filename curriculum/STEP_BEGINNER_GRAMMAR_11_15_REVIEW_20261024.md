## Teaching design

Each stage is aligned to the **actual original lesson section ID and order**, has a distinct Egyptian Arabic explanation, three English LTR sentence parts with **Arabic role captions**, one original model-written English example and Arabic translation, and a revealable optional mini-check with an explicit explanation. These are teaching aids and are **not** represented as protected source questions.

Existing original lesson content, practice questions, assessment answers, teacher-only bank annotations and student scores remain unchanged. Students with low English proficiency first learn the meaning, then the structure, then see the translated example, then interact.
