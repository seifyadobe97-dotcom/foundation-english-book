## Learning sequence — 6 Arabic-first editable stages / 24 authored examples
1. Modal followed by bare verb and function identification.
2. Present possibility (`may/might/could`) with readings that depend on context.
3. Negative uncertainty `might not`.
4. Strong present deduction `must be` vs `can't be` and not confusing `mustn't` prohibition.
5. Possible activity now `may/might be + V-ing`.
6. Past inference `must/might/couldn't have + V3` contrasted with past-regret constructions.

Three source-approved questions assigned **once** to stages 3, 5 and 6. Other stages have instructor-written examples, with no false claim of scored mastery coverage.
