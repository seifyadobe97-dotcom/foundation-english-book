# STEP with Seify — Question sourcing requirements

## Hard rule
All student-facing exercises and assessment items must have **four multiple-choice options, A/B/C/D**, and be traceable to a source previously provided by the project owner. No assistant-invented quizzes, distractors, free-response exercises, or item rewriting.

A teaching example inside the *explanation* may be author-written and clearly presented as an example; it is not an exercise.

Never publish an item without its bank ID, named source, page or model/question locator, original text, the four original options in original order, correct answer, editorial review, and Arabic rationales for every incorrect selection.

In the current foundation the 15 exercises are matched to the previously source-traced pilot bank in `supabase/functions/step-course/lesson-data.json`. The original PDF attachments were not accessible in this turn, so this is *traceability to the reviewed bank*, not a new page-by-page independent verification. A final source-text audit requires the actual uploaded original documents.

## QA requirements
- 100% of visible exercise stems, options, ordering and keys must match the original source register exactly.
- Source references must be displayed or auditable for every question.
- Arabic explanations may be instructor-authored but must distinguish why *that chosen option* fails from why the key succeeds.
- A/B/C/D choices and English sentences render left-to-right; the teaching scaffolding remains in beginner-friendly Arabic.
- Items of questionable accuracy, source or licensing must be quarantined until reviewed, never silently substituted.
- This is an independently developed STEP prep platform, not an official ETEC source or accredited test.
